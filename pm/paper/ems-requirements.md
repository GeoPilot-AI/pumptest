# Environmental Modelling & Software: what a submission needs

Read from the journal's guide for authors on 2026-10-14.

## Article type

**Research article** (the most common type). The guide asks that:
- the mathematical or algorithmic foundations of models or software are properly documented;
- performance is reported against data, with relevance to user needs;
- software usability, reliability, verification and validation are backed by quantitative results;
- development and maintenance costs, and adoption in the target user groups, are addressed;
- licensing and open-source access are clearly specified;
- objectives are stated, development steps reported with the rationale for the approach, and the work substantially tested; comparisons with alternative approaches are encouraged;
- historical progress on the problem is briefly reviewed, showing how the work adds value.

## Required pieces

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Abstract | At most 150 words: purpose, procedures, main findings, conclusions; no references |
| Keywords | 1 to 7 |
| Highlights | 3 to 5 bullets, each at most 85 characters including spaces; separate file |
| Graphical abstract | Required; at least 1328 px wide, readable at 13 cm; TIFF, EPS, PDF or Office |
| Software and/or data availability section | Name, developer and contact, year first available, hardware required, software required, availability and cost, program language, program size. "Contact the author" is not acceptable; use GitHub or similar. Software must be available to reviewers. |
| Research data | Option C: deposit data in a repository, cite and link it, or explain why not |
| Declarations | Competing interests (declarations tool), funding statement, CRediT author roles |
| Generative AI | A declaration section before the references if AI tools were used in preparing the manuscript |
| Figures | Separate files, 300 dpi minimum for raster images; colour accessible to colour-blind readers |
| Source | LaTeX (Elsevier template encouraged) or Word; not PDF |
| Sections | Numbered 1, 1.1, 1.1.1 |

## Preprint

Preprints do not count as prior publication. During submission the journal
offers free posting on SSRN, made public once the manuscript passes desk
review. Other preprint servers (EarthArXiv, ESS Open Archive) are allowed
under Elsevier's sharing policy.

## Before writing

Read several recent EMS research articles that present open-source modelling
software, to match their structure and the depth of evaluation reviewers expect.

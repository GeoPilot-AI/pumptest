# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

- Theis, Hantush-Jacob, Papadopulos-Cooper (skin, fitted casing radius) and
  Neuman solutions, by Laplace kernels and Stehfest inversion.
- Least-squares fitting over any number of wells, with standard errors,
  correlations, RMSE, AIC and AICc; `compare` ranks models by AICc.
- Pumping schedules by superposition: recovery, variable-rate and step tests.
- Cooper-Jacob, Theis recovery and step-drawdown methods.
- Bourdet derivative; unit conversion; water levels to drawdown.
- Seven published tests bundled in `pumptest.datasets`.
- Validation against AQTESOLV, Kruseman and de Ridder, and published
  type-curve tables.
